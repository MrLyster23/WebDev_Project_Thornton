About us: Wheels on Fire

Why cars?

John has loved cars since he was a child. Originally enamoured with their flair and speed, he eventually became intrested in how they ran, and got to work

After studying cars and honing his craft for years, John eventually worked as a mechanic for several famous luxury brands, but he always had a bigger dream

He always wanted to have his own car dealership, to guide his customers wisely, and be a piller of the community (and money!)
